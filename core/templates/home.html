About me

I'm a 7th-semester Information Technology undergraduate at the University of Gujrat with a solid base in software engineering fundamentals and relational database design. Day to day, I work at the intersection of two things: building the systems a business runs on, and making sense of the data those systems produce.

As an IT Automation & ERP developer

I build Django/ERP applications on Microsoft SQL Server and the automation scripts around them — things like the WhatsApp-integrated attendance system I run at Developer Zone, which replaced a manual check-in process with automated logging, leave workflows, and scheduled reporting.

As a data scientist

I use Pandas, NumPy, and Power BI to turn raw operational data into dashboards people actually check, and I train models with scikit-learn for practical, well-defined problems — classification, regression, and lately conversational interfaces over structured data.

Both sides feed each other: the systems I build generate the data I analyze, and what I learn from that data usually points back to something worth automating next.
